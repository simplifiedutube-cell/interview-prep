Base rates to calibrate against: ~40 applications per interview · median ~108 days from search start to first offer · loops run 2–8 weeks. This is why applications start week 1, not week 7 — gating them on readiness pushes the first offer past week 20.

Weekly outreach targets (block 4, ~10h/week):

Channel Volume/week Reply rate
Targeted cold email to named HMs/founders 25 15–25% ← the #1 channel for a candidate without brand names, and currently unused
LinkedIn DMs 15 ~17%
Referral asks 5–8 Referrals = 30–50% of hires from ~7% of applicants
Portal applications 10, capped 2–5% — volume filler only

Every outreach message carries one artifact link — the 90-second demo video, a README, or the blog post. That’s what converts.

Tier 0 — apply Day 1. Zero cooldown. Pays money. ⚡

Given <3 months runway, these come first. They’re free to fail, they’re high-fidelity rehearsal of exactly the resume-probing screen you’re weakest at, and they’re the 2026 resume line that makes Tier 2 reachable.

Cap contract work at 10h/week once accepted — it’s a bridge, not a career move.

⛔ Do not apply

Research Engineer / Research Scientist / Applied Scientist (paper discussion + implement-attention-from-memory ends it in 20 minutes) · ML Engineer at fintech/adtech (recsys funnels, DCNv2/MMoE, feature stores — 8+ weeks to become mediocre) · ML Platform / Inference Infra / MLOps-heavy (punished on K8s/Ray/Triton, the wrong axis) · Nvidia (CUDA, hardware-software co-design) · Perplexity (Kubernetes debugging is a decision round).

Loop-week protocol

In any week with a live onsite: cut study to 4h/day and shift the whole day 3–4 hours later. Each onsite costs ~1.5 study days — half a day of company-specific prep, the interview itself, and a recovery morning. Don’t try to do both.

India SWE median TC ₹29.7L · p75 ₹48.6L · p90 ₹69.9L · Hyderabad/Bengaluru ₹34.9L. Senior AI Engineer at 6–9 yrs: ₹42–66L Hyderabad-indexed, ₹48–72L Bangalore. AI Engineer carries a 40–60% premium over Backend.

Give one number, not a range — a range is heard as its floor. Decline current CTC, give expected. Ask EOR vs contractor vs GCC entity in round 1 — it determines equity eligibility, comp and tax.
